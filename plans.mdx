Proof of Human offers three self-serve plans. You can choose a plan when you sign up and change it at any time on the [Plan page](https://app.poh.org/account/plan) of your account.

## Comparing plans

|  | Free | Pay as you go | Academic |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Price** | Free, no card needed | $0.25 per session, no base fee | $299 per month |
| **Included sessions** | Up to 100,000 per month | Billed per session | 10,000 per month |
| **Additional sessions** | Not collected (see [Monthly session limit](#monthly-session-limit-on-the-free-plan)) | $0.25 per session | $0.03 per session from 10,001 to 100,000, then $0.01 per session above 100,000 |
| **Home dashboard** | ✓ | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Route and tag summaries** | Totals only | Full breakdowns | Full breakdowns |
| **Sessions, Tags, and Visitor IDs pages** | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Session details** (risk explanation, action log, checks) | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **[API access](/accessing-session-data)** | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| **Who can sign up** | Anyone | Anyone | `.edu` email addresses only |

Paid plans are billed monthly for the sessions you use. For custom volumes or an enterprise agreement, contact us at support@poh.org.

## What's included on the Free plan

The Free plan runs the full Proof of Human tracker on your site, so every session is scored the same way as on a paid plan. You see the results in aggregate on the home dashboard and in the totals at the top of each route and tag summary.

The Sessions, Tags, and Visitor IDs pages, the detailed breakdowns in route and tag summaries (bot detection, device and network checks, repeat visitors, recent history, and locations), session details, and the [API](/accessing-session-data) are only available on Pay as you go and Academic plans. Free accounts don't have API keys. API requests from a Free account return `403` with the message `API access is not available on the free plan - please upgrade your plan`.

### Monthly session limit on the Free plan

Free accounts can collect up to **100,000 sessions per month**. The month starts on the day and time you signed up (in UTC), not on the 1st of the calendar month. For example, an account created on March 14 resets on the 14th of every month. Accounts created on the 29th, 30th, or 31st reset on the last day of shorter months.

<Warning>
  Once you reach the limit, new sessions are not collected until your next month starts. The tracker still loads on your pages, but those sessions are not recorded, scored, or shown on the dashboard, and they cannot be recovered later. Sessions already in progress when you reach the limit are recorded in full. If you expect more than 100,000 sessions a month, upgrade before you launch.
</Warning>

### Sessions collected on the Free plan stay locked

Session details for sessions collected while your account is on the Free plan are never available, even after you upgrade. After upgrading:

- Sessions collected on a paid plan show full details on the dashboard and through the API.
- Sessions collected on the Free plan stay locked. The API leaves them out of the [user](/api-reference/user-data) and [tag](/api-reference/tag-data) endpoints, and the [session](/api-reference/session-data) and [events](/api-reference/events-data) endpoints return `403` with the message `Session was collected on the free plan - details are not available`.

If you need session-level data for a study or launch, switch to a paid plan before you start collecting.

## Changing plans

Account admins can change plans on the [Plan page](https://app.poh.org/account/plan). Changes take effect immediately.

- **Free to a paid plan**: you are asked to add a card, and your subscription starts as soon as it's saved. Sessions collected from then on include full details.
- **Between paid plans**: your new subscription starts right away and the old one is canceled.
- **Paid plan to Free**: your subscription is canceled right away and you are billed for the sessions used so far. Monthly fees are not refunded for the unused part of the month. Free plan limits apply from that moment on.
